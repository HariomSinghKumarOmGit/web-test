Men's Footwear Size Guide

Not sure which size to pick? Measure your foot length at the end of the day (feet swell slightly through the day), match it against the size chart on each product page, and leave a thumb's width of space at the toe. Walkaroo shoes for men run true to standard Indian/UK sizing — if you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for sports shoes and true-to-size for formal shoes.

How to Choose the Right Footwear for Men

1. Match the shoe to your routine

Sneakers and slip-ons work best for daily wear, sports shoes for fitness, and formal shoes for the workplace.

2. Check the materials

Look for breathable mesh uppers, EVA or PU cushioning, and anti-slip outsoles for lasting comfort.

3. Get the fit right

Refer to the size chart on each product page and measure your feet before ordering shoes for men online.

4. Shop for the season

Choose rain shoes for monsoon, breathable sandals and sliders for summer, and covered sneakers or shoes for winter.
